  Layer 1: Regulatory Knowledge Base

  Layer 2: Obligation Registry
    → Structured rules derived FROM the documents
    → "What must be done, by when, by whom?"

  Layer 3: Company Context
    → Client profile (listed/unlisted, paid-up capital, turnover, type)

  The magic happens at Layer 2 + Layer 3 intersection. Most competitors only

  3. Here's the critical design question:

  How do obligations get created?

  Option A — Manual curation:
  You (or a CS expert) reads circulars and manually creates structured
  obligations. High accuracy, doesn't scale.

  Option B — LLM extraction:
  Your system reads a new circular and auto-extracts obligations from it.
  Scales, but needs validation.

  Option C — Hybrid:
  LLM proposes obligations from new circulars, a human reviewer approves them
  before they go live.

  Which approach are you leaning toward? This is probably the single biggest
  architectural decision for the product.
