### GST Annual Return (GSTR-9/9C) for FY 2024-25
**Circular Reference:** GSTN | FAQ on GSTR-9/9C for FY 2024-25 | Published October 2025

**In Brief:** The annual return forms for FY 2024-25 introduce new tables, revised ITC auto-population logic, and a separate late fee for GSTR-9C. Key changes involve handling ITC claims, reversals, and reclaims across financial years, and how these are reflected in various tables of GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C.

## Key Changes

*   **GSTR-9C E-commerce Reporting:** Separate reporting is now required for supplies where tax is paid by the e-commerce operator.
*   **Table 11 — Cash and ITC:** Table 11 of Form GSTR-9C updated to report additional tax payment made due to turnover mismatch, via cash as well as ITC. Earlier this only mentioned payment via cash.
*   **New Table 17 — Late Fee for GSTR-9C:** A new Table 17 has been inserted in Form GSTR-9C to calculate the late fee on filing of Form GSTR-9C after filing of Form GSTR-9. Late fees are calculated from the date of filing GSTR-9 or the due date of filing the annual return, whichever is later, until the filing of GSTR-9C.
*   **ITC Reversal and Reclaim Reporting:** ITC pertaining to FY 2023-24 claimed in FY 2024-25 (excluding reclaims under Rule 37/37A) must be reported in Table 6A1. ITC claimed, reversed, and reclaimed within FY 2024-25 follows the 6B → 7 → 6H sequence. ITC reclaimed under Rule 37/37A is treated as ITC of the year of reclaim and reported in Table 6H — not 6A1.

## Action Items

1.  Ensure all GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns for FY 2024-25 are filed. Portal access to Form GSTR-9/Form GSTR-9C is blocked otherwise.
2.  All eligible ITC and credit notes adjustments for FY 2024-25 should be claimed in Form GSTR-3B of October 2025.
3.  Reporting of ITC availed and reversed must be matched with monthly GST returns and books of accounts.
4.  Use the latest offline utility and schema for uploading returns.

## Exceptions & Thresholds

*   GSTR-9/9C enablement requires all GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns to be filed first.
*   65% concessional rate checkbox removed from Tables 17 and 18 for FY 2024-25.

## Additional Notes

These changes align the ITC reporting in Form GSTR-3B and ITC appearing in Form GSTR-2B with the reporting to be made in Form GSTR-9, creating a common basis for automated ITC reconciliation.

The changes reflect the Government's focus on ensuring data accuracy and reducing manual adjustments. While it improves transparency, it also requires increased compliance standards, making it essential for taxpayers to keep their data clean and consistent every month.
