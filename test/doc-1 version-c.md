### Section 128A Clarifications: GSTR-3B Payments and Partial Period Demands
**Circular Reference:** CBIC | Circular No. 248/05/2025-GST and Notification No. 11/2025–Central Tax | March 27, 2025

**In Brief:** CBIC clarifies eligibility for Section 128A benefits (waiver of interest/penalty on Section 73 demands for July 2017-March 2020). Taxpayers who paid via GSTR-3B before November 1, 2024, are eligible. Also, taxpayers with demands spanning both the covered period and other periods can settle only the covered portion without withdrawing the entire appeal. These changes address industry concerns and ensure consistent application of the law.

## Key Changes

*   **GSTR-3B Payments Eligible:** Taxpayers who paid tax demanded via GSTR-3B before November 1, 2024, can avail of Section 128A benefits, subject to verification by the proper officer. After November 1, 2024, payments must be made via FORM GST DRC-03 as per Rule 164.
*   **Partial Period Demands:** If a notice/order covers periods both within and outside Section 128A's scope (July 2017-March 2020), taxpayers can now pay the tax liability only for the covered period and intimate the appellate authority/tribunal that they will not pursue the appeal for that period. The appellate authority will then pass orders for the remaining period as deemed appropriate. This modifies the earlier requirement to withdraw the entire appeal.
*   **Rule 164 Amendments:** Rule 164(4) and Rule 164(7) are amended to reflect the above change, allowing taxpayers to file FORM SPL-01 or FORM SPL-02 after paying the tax liability for the periods covered under section 128A.
*   **Withdrawal of Prior Clarification:** Clarification issued vide point 6 of the Table at para 4 of circular No. 238/32/2024-GST dated 15th October 2024 is withdrawn.

## Action Items

1.  **Identify Clients:** Identify clients with demands under Section 73 for July 2017-March 2020.
2.  **Review Payment Method:** For clients who paid via GSTR-3B before November 1, 2024, verify eligibility for Section 128A benefits.
3.  **File FORM SPL-01/02:** For eligible clients, file FORM SPL-01 or FORM SPL-02 to avail the benefit of Section 128A.
4.  **Intimate Appellate Authority:** Where demands cover periods both within and outside Section 128A, intimate the appellate authority/tribunal of the intent to avail the benefit of Section 128A and not pursue the appeal for the covered period.
5.  **Track Order:** Monitor the appellate authority/tribunal for orders pertaining to the period not covered under Section 128A.

## Exceptions & Thresholds

*   The benefit of paying through GSTR-3B applies only to payments made before November 1, 2024.
*   For payments made on or after November 1, 2024, Rule 164 mandates payment through FORM GST DRC-03.

## Additional Notes

*   The circular refers to FORM SPL-01 and FORM SPL-02, but does not define them. CAs should check for updates on the GST portal regarding these forms.
*   The proper officer will verify eligibility for Section 128A benefits for those who paid via GSTR-3B. Ensure clients have documentation ready to support their claim.
*   The amendment to Rule 164(7) allows taxpayers to settle demands partially, which means composite demand appellants can now settle one period without abandoning the other.
