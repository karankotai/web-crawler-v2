# All Evaluation Questions

## Blind Test Questions

1. What are the key compliance deadlines for merchant bankers under the January 2026 SEBI amendment?
2. Can an insurer raise health insurance premiums for senior citizens by 15% in a single year?
3. As a Tier 4 Urban Cooperative Bank, can we provide working capital loans secured by gold to a jewellery manufacturer?
4. How quickly must an insurance company report a cyber incident to IRDAI, and what log retention period is required?
5. What specific changes were made to the NCS Master Circular for green debt security reviewer appointments?
6. We are a gold jewellery manufacturing NBFC-factor. Can we pledge our gold raw material inventory with a scheduled commercial bank to get working capital finance?
7. As an NBFC, do we need to screen our customers against the UN sanctions list? What specific obligations apply to us under the latest RBI circular on UAPA compliance?
8. Our company received a GST demand order covering the period July 2017 to March 2021 and we filed an appeal. Under the recent CGST Rules amendment, can we withdraw the appeal only for the 2017-2020 portion and continue fighting the 2020-2021 portion?
9. Our listed NBFC wants to issue green bonds to fund renewable energy projects. What does SEBI require regarding the appointment of an independent reviewer for our green debt issuance?
10. We are an NBFC hiring a merchant banker for our upcoming public issue of NCDs. The merchant banker's group entity holds a 3% stake in our company. Are there any new SEBI disclosure requirements that apply?
11. Can our NBFC reduce the interest rate spread for an existing corporate borrower before 3 years to prevent them from refinancing with a competitor bank?
12. Our company has a floating rate term loan from an NBFC. At the next interest rate reset, can we request to switch to a fixed rate instead? What are the rules around this?

### Multi-Regulator Questions

13. Our listed NBFC wants to raise funds by issuing green NCDs to finance our renewable energy lending portfolio. At the same time, we're losing corporate borrowers to competitor banks and want to reduce interest rate spreads to retain them. What are the regulatory requirements we need to satisfy across SEBI and RBI?
14. We are a listed gold jewellery manufacturer. We have working capital loans secured by our gold inventory from SBI, a pending GST appeal for demand orders covering 2018-2020, and we're planning to issue green NCDs to fund our transition to recycled gold sourcing. What are the compliance requirements we need to track across RBI, GST, and SEBI?
15. Our mid-sized company has floating rate loans from three different banks approaching reset dates, and our HR team is getting complaints from senior employees about steep health insurance premium increases from our group insurer. Are there any recent regulatory changes that help us on both fronts?
16. Our NBFC is preparing a public NCD issue through a Category I merchant banker, and we just received the latest RBI circular updating the UNSC sanctions list. Our compliance officer wants to know: what are all the overlapping obligations we need to handle right now from both SEBI and RBI?
