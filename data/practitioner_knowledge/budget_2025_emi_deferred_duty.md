# Budget 2025 EMI-Based Deferred Duty Payment Scheme

## Overview

The Union Budget 2025-26 introduced an EMI-based deferred duty payment scheme for customs duties. This allows eligible importers to pay customs duty in monthly instalments (EMIs) instead of upfront at the time of import clearance. The scheme is designed to ease working capital pressure on manufacturers who import raw materials and capital goods.

## Eligibility — Who Qualifies?

### Eligible Entity Categories

1. **Manufacturers** registered under the Factories Act, 1948 — importing raw materials, intermediate goods, or capital goods for use in their own manufacturing
2. **Authorised Economic Operators (AEO)** — importers with AEO-T2 or AEO-T3 certification from CBIC
3. **Status Holders** under Foreign Trade Policy — exporters with One Star to Five Star Export House status
4. **SEZ/EOU units** — Special Economic Zone and Export Oriented Units importing for manufacturing and export

### NOT Eligible — Traders

- **A trader (not a manufacturer) is NOT eligible** for the EMI-based deferred duty payment scheme
- The scheme is specifically designed for entities that import goods for use in manufacturing or processing
- Pure trading entities (buy and resell without value addition) do not qualify
- The rationale: The scheme aims to support domestic manufacturing by reducing upfront duty burden on inputs — traders who simply resell imported goods do not contribute to this objective

### Other Exclusions

- Importers with adverse compliance history (pending demands, penalties, or prosecution)
- Importers not registered on ICEGATE
- Goods subject to anti-dumping duty, countervailing duty, or safeguard duty (these must be paid upfront)
- Personal imports and baggage

## Scheme Details

### EMI Structure

- **Number of instalments**: Up to 3 monthly instalments (spread over 90 days from import date)
- **First instalment**: 40% of total duty payable at the time of clearance
- **Second instalment**: 30% due within 30 days of clearance
- **Third instalment**: 30% due within 60 days of clearance
- **Interest**: No interest charged if EMIs are paid on time; delayed payment attracts interest under Section 47(2) of Customs Act

### How to Avail

1. Register on ICEGATE with AEO/manufacturer credentials
2. At the time of filing Bill of Entry, select "Deferred Duty Payment" option
3. Pay the first instalment (40%) to clear goods
4. System automatically generates EMI schedule for remaining instalments
5. Pay subsequent instalments through ICEGATE e-payment facility

### Security/Bond Requirement

- Importer must furnish a bond/bank guarantee for the deferred amount
- Bond value: 100% of the deferred duty amount
- Bank guarantee: 25% of the deferred duty amount (for AEO importers: reduced to 10%)
