# Cross-Regulatory: RBI Floating Rate Lending and GST on Bundled Insurance with Loans

## Overview

Two concurrent regulatory changes affect banks offering home loans with bundled life insurance:
1. **RBI**: Mandated shift to floating-rate lending for certain loan categories, with transparent EMI reset mechanisms
2. **GST Council**: Changed treatment of insurance premiums, including those bundled with loans

This document analyzes how these two changes interact for a bank offering a home loan with bundled life insurance.

## RBI Floating-Rate Lending Mandate

### Key RBI Directions

**RBI Circular on Reset of Floating Interest Rate (August 2023)** and subsequent amendments:

1. **Mandatory periodic reset**: For all EMI-based floating-rate retail loans (housing, vehicle, personal), banks must reset EMI or tenure at pre-defined intervals (typically annually)
2. **Borrower choice**: At each reset, borrower can choose between:
   - Increased EMI (same tenure)
   - Extended tenure (same EMI)
   - Combination of both
3. **Negative amortization prohibited**: Banks cannot allow a situation where EMI doesn't cover even the interest portion
4. **Communication requirement**: Banks must inform borrowers of the exact impact on EMI/tenure within 30 days of repo rate change

### Impact on EMI Structure

For a home loan with bundled life insurance:
- The **EMI comprises**: Principal repayment + Interest + Insurance premium component
- When interest rate changes (repo rate-linked), the interest component changes
- The insurance premium component may or may not change (depends on the insurance policy terms)
- **EMI reset calculation** must transparently show:
  - Revised interest component (based on new floating rate)
  - Insurance premium component (may be fixed or variable)
  - Principal repayment portion

## GST on Insurance Premiums Bundled with Loans

### Current GST Treatment

1. **Insurance premium**: Life insurance premiums are taxable at **18% GST** (SAC 997132)
2. **Loan processing fee**: Taxable at **18% GST** as a financial service
3. **Interest on loan**: **Exempt from GST** (Entry 27 of Exemption Notification 12/2017-Central Tax Rate)

### Bundled Insurance — Composite or Mixed Supply?

When a bank offers a home loan with **mandatory** bundled life insurance:

**If insurance is mandatory** (condition for loan disbursement):
- This is a **composite supply** under Section 2(30) of CGST Act
- The principal supply is the loan (financial service)
- Insurance is ancillary to the loan
- **GST treatment**: The interest (principal supply) is exempt → the entire composite supply is exempt from GST
- **However**: IRDAI and RBI have both discouraged mandatory bundling — if insurance is truly mandatory, the bank may face regulatory objections

**If insurance is optional** (offered but not required):
- This is NOT a composite supply — each component is a separate supply
- Loan interest: Exempt from GST
- Insurance premium: Taxable at 18% GST
- The bank/insurer must issue a **separate GST invoice** for the insurance premium

### GST Invoice to the Borrower

**(a) EMI structure impact**:
- The floating rate reset changes only the interest component of EMI
- Insurance premium within EMI is a separate identifiable charge
- After rate reset, the bank must show the revised EMI breakup: interest (exempt) + insurance premium (18% GST) + principal repayment
- The GST on insurance is charged to the borrower as part of the insurance premium — not as part of interest

**(b) GST invoice requirements**:
- The bank (acting as corporate agent for the insurer) OR the insurance company directly must issue a GST invoice for the insurance premium
- Invoice must show: Insurance premium (base amount) + GST at 18%
- If the bank collects premium on behalf of the insurer, it acts as an agent — the insurer issues the invoice
- The borrower does NOT get ITC on the insurance premium (residential home loan is for personal use, not business)
- If the borrower is a business entity (commercial property loan), ITC on insurance premium is blocked under Section 17(5)(b) unless it's a statutory obligation

### Interaction of the Two Changes

| Aspect | RBI Impact | GST Impact |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| EMI calculation | Floating rate → EMI changes periodically | Insurance GST component stays at 18% (unless rate reduced) |
| EMI communication | Must show breakup transparently | Must separate exempt (interest) from taxable (insurance) |
| Borrower cost | Interest may increase/decrease with repo rate | Insurance GST is fixed cost (18% of premium) |
| Invoice | Bank issues EMI statement showing breakup | Insurer/bank issues separate GST invoice for insurance |
| ITC for borrower | N/A (RBI matter) | Not available for residential; blocked under S17(5) for commercial |
