# Section 128A Amnesty Scheme - Portal and Procedure Guide

## Overview

Section 128A of the CGST Act (inserted by Finance Act 2024, effective from specified date via notification) provides conditional waiver of interest and penalty for demand notices issued under Section 73 for FY 2017-18, 2018-19, and 2019-20. The taxpayer must pay the full tax demand (and file an application in SPL-01) to get interest and penalty waived.

## Eligibility Conditions

1. **Applicable to Section 73 demands only** — NOT Section 74 (fraud/willful misstatement/suppression)
2. **Financial years**: Only FY 2017-18, 2018-19, 2019-20
3. **Tax must be paid in full** — no partial payment option for the tax component
4. **Application deadline**: As notified (check latest notification for the exact date)
5. **Pending appeal**: If taxpayer has filed an appeal, it must be withdrawn before applying

## Payment and Filing Procedure

### Step 1: Calculate the Tax Amount

- Tax demand as per the order under Section 73 (DRC-07)
- If the demand has been partially paid, calculate the balance tax payable
- Interest and penalty components are waived — do NOT pay them

### Step 2: Make Payment via DRC-03

**Important**: Payment must be made through **DRC-03** (voluntary payment form) on the GST portal.

- Navigate to: Services → User Services → My Applications → DRC-03
- Select the cause: "Voluntary" payment
- Enter the demand order reference (DRC-07 number)
- Pay the exact tax amount (CGST + SGST/UTGST or IGST as applicable)
- **Use electronic cash ledger** — ITC cannot be used for this payment

### Step 3: File SPL-01 Application

- **SPL-01** is the specific application form for Section 128A waiver
- Navigate to: Services → User Services → My Applications → select "Application for Waiver of Interest and Penalty under Section 128A"
- Enter the DRC-07 order details (order number, date, tax period)
- Attach proof of tax payment (DRC-03 acknowledgment, challan)
- Submit the form

### Step 4: Processing

- The proper officer verifies the payment and application
- If satisfied, issues order in **SPL-02** (waiver order)
- If deficient, may issue notice for additional information
- No hearing is required if the application is in order

## Known Portal Issues

### 1. SPL-01 Form Not Visible

**Problem**: Some taxpayers report that the SPL-01 option is not appearing under "My Applications."

**Possible Causes**:
- The form may be available only after the effective date notification is issued
- The taxpayer's registration may be in a state (cancelled/suspended) that blocks access
- Browser cache issue

**Workaround**:
- Clear browser cache and try in incognito mode
- Ensure you're using Chrome or Firefox (Edge has compatibility issues)
- Check if the feature has been enabled for your state (phased rollout possible)

### 2. DRC-03 vs GSTR-3B Payment Confusion

**Problem**: Some taxpayers paid the demand through GSTR-3B instead of DRC-03. Question: Does GSTR-3B payment qualify for Section 128A benefit?

**Analysis**:
- Section 128A requires payment of "tax" — it does not specify the payment mechanism
- However, the SPL-01 form requires DRC-03 reference for linking
- **Practical advice**: If already paid via GSTR-3B, file DRC-03 with zero amount and reference the GSTR-3B payment. Alternatively, wait for clarificatory circular.
- CBIC has clarified in FAQ that payment through any mode should be acceptable, but portal linkage remains an issue

### 3. Partial Demand Coverage

**Problem**: Demand order covers multiple issues (some under Section 73, some under Section 74). Can Section 128A be applied to the Section 73 portion?

**Position**: Yes, if the demand order is divisible, the Section 73 portion can be settled under 128A. The Section 74 portion must be dealt with separately. However, the portal may not support splitting — may need manual filing with jurisdictional officer.

## Rule 164 of CGST Rules

Rule 164 prescribes the procedure for availing the waiver under Section 128A:
- Form SPL-01 to be filed electronically
- Payment proof (DRC-03) to be attached
- Proper officer to verify within 60 days (or as prescribed)
- Order in Form SPL-02 for acceptance, SPL-03 for rejection

## SPL-02 Process (Settlement/Waiver Order)

### What is SPL-02?

SPL-02 is the order form issued by the proper officer granting the waiver of interest and penalty under Section 128A. The taxpayer does NOT file SPL-02 — it is issued by the officer after processing SPL-01.

### SPL-02 Process Flow

1. Taxpayer files SPL-01 with tax payment proof (DRC-03)
2. Proper officer examines the application
3. If application is in order:
   - Officer issues **SPL-02** (Waiver Order) confirming interest and penalty waiver
   - SPL-02 is available under: Services → User Services → View Additional Notices and Orders
4. If application has deficiency:
   - Officer issues notice for additional information/correction
   - Taxpayer responds within the specified time
5. If application is rejected:
   - Officer issues **SPL-03** (Rejection Order) with reasons
   - Taxpayer can rectify and re-apply if within the deadline

### Partial Settlement of Demand

For a demand order that was **partially confirmed and partially dropped**:
- The taxpayer can settle only the **confirmed portion** under Section 128A
- Pay the full tax of the confirmed portion via DRC-03
- File SPL-01 referencing only the confirmed tax amount
- The dropped portion does not require any action
- In the SPL-01 form, enter the confirmed tax amount (not the original full demand)

## Section 74 to Section 73 Rectification — Portal Workaround

### The Problem

When a demand order was originally issued under **Section 74** (fraud/suppression) and later **rectified to Section 73** (normal demand), the GST portal may show an error when filing SPL-01. This happens because the system still flags the order as a Section 74 order.

### Official Workaround per CBIC Circular

**CBIC Circular No. 238/32/2024-GST** provides the following guidance:

1. If the original order was issued under Section 74 and later rectified/modified to Section 73, the taxpayer IS eligible for Section 128A benefit
2. **Portal limitation**: The portal may not automatically update the section reference after rectification
3. **Workaround**:
   - File the application **manually** with the jurisdictional proper officer
   - Attach the rectification order showing the change from Section 74 to Section 73
   - The proper officer will process the application offline
   - Alternatively, use **FORM GST SPL-05** or **SPL-06** as applicable and mention the rectification details in the remarks column (Column 19/20)
4. The GSTN portal team has been instructed to enable system-level correction for such cases, but until then, manual filing is the accepted route

### Important Clarifications

- The date of the **original Section 74 order** does not matter — what matters is that the order is now under Section 73 post-rectification
- If only a portion of the order was rectified from S74 to S73, only that portion qualifies for 128A
- The taxpayer should retain a copy of both the original order and the rectification order as evidence

## Practical Tips for CAs

1. **Maintain a tracker** of all Section 73 orders for FY 2017-18 to 2019-20
2. Calculate whether it's beneficial to pay under 128A or continue the appeal (if penalty is small, litigation may be more expensive)
3. If the taxpayer has a strong case on merits, evaluate whether paying tax under 128A is worth it vs. winning the appeal and paying nothing
4. **Deadline awareness**: The scheme has a limited window — track the notification date closely
5. For composite demands (73 + 74), get a written communication from the officer about the split
6. For Section 74→73 rectified orders, keep the rectification order handy and use the manual filing route if the portal shows errors
