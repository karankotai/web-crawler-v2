# Inverted Duty ITC Refund — Rule 89(5) and Section 54(3) Complete Guide

## Overview

When the rate of tax on inputs is higher than the rate on output supplies (inverted duty structure), accumulated Input Tax Credit (ITC) builds up because output tax liability is less than input tax credit. Section 54(3) of the CGST Act allows refund of such accumulated ITC, and Rule 89(5) of the CGST Rules prescribes the formula for calculating the maximum refund amount.

## Eligibility Conditions under Section 54(3)

### Who Can Claim

1. **Any registered person** who has accumulated ITC due to the rate of tax on inputs being higher than the rate on output supplies
2. Applies to **manufacturers and traders** alike — the key criterion is inverted duty, not the nature of the entity
3. The accumulation must be due to **rate inversion** — not due to export (which has a separate refund mechanism)

### Conditions for Eligibility

1. **Inverted rated supplies only**: The refund is available only for supplies where input tax rate exceeds output tax rate — NOT for exempt supplies
2. **No refund on input services**: The refund under inverted duty is ONLY for ITC accumulated on inputs (goods), NOT on input services (as per the amendment via Notification No. 09/2022-CT dated July 5, 2022, and Supreme Court ruling in UOI v. VKC Footsteps)
3. **Refund period**: Refund can be claimed for any tax period. Application must be filed within **2 years** from the end of the financial year in which the claim arises
4. **No refund if supplies become exempt**: If the output supply is fully exempt from GST, Section 54(3) inverted duty refund does not apply
5. **Compensation cess**: Accumulated ITC of compensation cess due to inverted duty is NOT eligible for refund (only CGST/SGST/IGST ITC is refundable)

## Formula under Rule 89(5)

### The Formula

**Maximum Refund Amount** = (Turnover of inverted rated supply of goods and services × Net ITC ÷ Adjusted Total Turnover) − Tax payable on such inverted rated supply of goods and services

Where:
- **Turnover of inverted rated supply**: Value of inverted rated supplies made during the period (excluding exempt and nil-rated supplies)
- **Net ITC**: ITC availed on inputs during the period (NOT input services, NOT capital goods)
- **Adjusted Total Turnover**: Total turnover (excluding exempt supplies + taxes) during the period
- **Tax payable on inverted rated supply**: Output tax liability on the inverted rated supplies

### Worked Example

Manufacturer produces goods taxable at 5% GST using raw materials taxable at 18% GST:
- Turnover of inverted rated supply: Rs. 50,00,000
- Net ITC on inputs: Rs. 4,50,000 (18% on Rs. 25,00,000 inputs)
- Adjusted Total Turnover: Rs. 55,00,000
- Tax payable on inverted rated supply: Rs. 2,50,000 (5% on Rs. 50,00,000)

Maximum Refund = (50,00,000 × 4,50,000 / 55,00,000) − 2,50,000 = Rs. 4,09,091 − Rs. 2,50,000 = **Rs. 1,59,091**

## Documentary Requirements

### Application Form

File **Form GST RFD-01** electronically on the GST portal:
- Navigate to: Services → Refunds → Application for Refund
- Select refund type: "Refund of ITC on account of inverted tax structure"
- Select the tax period for which refund is claimed

### Documents to Attach

1. **Statement 1A**: Details of inverted rated supplies (auto-populated from GSTR-1/3B)
2. **Declaration**: That ITC claimed as refund has not been availed on excluded supplies (input services, capital goods)
3. **Undertaking**: That the amount of refund claimed does not exceed the maximum refund amount as per Rule 89(5) formula
4. **CA certificate/Auditor's report**: Required for refund claims exceeding **Rs. 2 lakh** — certificate confirming that the incidence of tax has not been passed on to another person
5. **Copy of GSTR-2A/2B**: Showing ITC details for the period (for verification by officer)
6. **Bank account details**: For credit of refund amount (must be linked to the GSTIN)

### Processing Timeline

- Provisional refund: 90% of the claimed amount issued within **7 days** of acknowledgment (for non-risky exporters)
- Final order: Within **60 days** of receipt of complete application
- If deficiency: Notice issued in **RFD-03** within 15 days; taxpayer responds in RFD-04

## Common Rejections and How to Avoid

1. **Including input services in Net ITC**: Only ITC on inputs (goods) is eligible — ensure you exclude service ITC
2. **Wrong period selection**: Refund must be claimed for a specific tax period — cannot be cumulative across multiple periods in one application
3. **Exceeding the formula amount**: The refund claimed cannot exceed the formula result; even if accumulated ITC is higher, the formula caps the refund
4. **Unjust enrichment**: If the manufacturer has passed the tax burden to customers (charged higher price), refund may be denied under the unjust enrichment clause
