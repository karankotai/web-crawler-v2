# NBFC UAPA Sanctions Screening and STR Filing Obligations

## Overview

When an NBFC registered with RBI onboards a new customer, it must comply with UAPA (Unlawful Activities Prevention Act) screening, UN sanctions list verification, and ongoing transaction monitoring obligations. This document specifically covers the obligations for NBFCs (as distinct from scheduled commercial banks), including STR filing with FIU-IND.

## Are NBFC Obligations Different from Scheduled Commercial Banks?

**No material difference**. Per RBI's regulatory framework, the UAPA/sanctions screening and STR obligations apply uniformly to ALL **Regulated Entities (REs)**, which includes:
- Scheduled Commercial Banks
- Urban Co-operative Banks
- **NBFCs** (all categories — NBFC-ND, NBFC-D, NBFC-MFI, NBFC-ICC, NBFC-Factor, etc.)
- Payment System Operators
- Housing Finance Companies

The RBI Master Direction on KYC (updated periodically) and UAPA circulars use the term "Regulated Entities" — making no distinction between banks and NBFCs for these obligations.

## UAPA and UN Sanctions Screening Obligations

### At Customer Onboarding

Per RBI circular RBI/2025-26/94 (DOR.AML.REC.58/14.06.001/2025-26) dated October 24, 2025:

1. **Screen against UNSC Sanctions Lists**: The NBFC must ensure that the customer (individual or entity) does not appear on:
   - UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List
   - UNSC 1988 Taliban Sanctions List
   - Other designated lists as notified by MHA
2. **Screen against domestic lists**: Check the UAPA list maintained by MHA (Ministry of Home Affairs) — individuals/organizations declared as terrorists/unlawful associations
3. **Follow the UAPA Order procedure**: As laid down in the UAPA Order dated February 2, 2021 (amended April 22, 2024)

### Ongoing Monitoring

1. **Periodic re-screening**: NBFCs must re-screen their entire customer database whenever the UNSC sanctions lists are updated (RBI circulates updates periodically)
2. **Transaction monitoring**: Monitor for suspicious patterns indicating terrorist financing or money laundering
3. **Immediate freeze**: If a match is found, immediately freeze the account/transaction and report to:
   - Joint Secretary (CTCR), Ministry of Home Affairs
   - RBI's Financial Intelligence Unit as applicable

### De-listing Requests

If a customer claims they are wrongly listed:
- Forward de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA
- Requests can also be submitted to the UN Ombudsperson
- The NBFC must NOT unfreeze the account until formal de-listing confirmation is received

## STR (Suspicious Transaction Report) Filing with FIU-IND

### Does the NBFC Need to File STRs?

**Yes, absolutely.** NBFCs are required to file Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) with the **Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND)** under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA).

### Legal Basis

1. **Section 12 of PMLA**: Every reporting entity (including NBFCs) must furnish information about suspicious transactions to FIU-IND
2. **RBI Master Direction on KYC**: Paragraph 64 requires all REs including NBFCs to file STRs
3. **PMLA Rules**: Rule 7 prescribes the STR format and timelines

### When to File STR

File an STR when any transaction (attempted or executed):
- Gives rise to a reasonable ground of suspicion that it involves proceeds of crime
- Appears to involve terrorist financing or is related to a designated entity
- Is unusually complex with no apparent economic rationale
- Is inconsistent with the customer's known profile (KYC data)

### STR Filing Process for NBFCs

1. **Internal escalation**: Branch/relationship manager escalates suspicion to the Principal Compliance Officer (PCO) / Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)
2. **Analysis**: PCO/MLRO evaluates the transaction against the customer profile
3. **Filing**: If suspicion is confirmed, file STR on the **FINnet portal** (https://finnet.gov.in) within **7 working days** of forming the suspicion
4. **No tipping off**: The NBFC must NOT inform the customer that an STR has been filed (Section 66 PMLA — tipping off is a criminal offence)
5. **Record keeping**: Maintain records of all STRs filed for a minimum of **5 years** from the date of filing

### Other Transaction Reports

In addition to STRs, NBFCs must file:
- **CTR (Cash Transaction Report)**: For cash transactions exceeding **Rs. 10 lakh** (or equivalent) in a month — file by 15th of the succeeding month
- **CCR (Counterfeit Currency Report)**: Within 7 working days
- **NPR (Non-Profit Organization Transaction Report)**: For transactions involving NPOs above prescribed thresholds

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- **Under PMLA**: Fines up to Rs. 5 lakh per default; imprisonment for directors/officers in serious cases
- **Under RBI directions**: Monetary penalty under Section 45MA of RBI Act; restriction on business activities
- **Reputational risk**: Non-compliance with UAPA/sanctions can lead to blacklisting
