# UCB Gold Lending — Tier-Wise Restrictions and RBI Master Direction

## Overview

Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs) are classified into 4 tiers based on their deposit size. The RBI Master Direction on Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral, along with its amendments, prescribes specific restrictions for UCBs based on their tier. This document covers the specific rules applicable to Tier 4 UCBs offering gold loans to jewellery manufacturers.

## UCB Tier Classification

| Tier | Deposit Size |
|------|-------------|
| Tier 1 | Up to Rs. 100 crore |
| Tier 2 | Rs. 100 crore to Rs. 1,000 crore |
| Tier 3 | Rs. 1,000 crore to Rs. 10,000 crore |
| Tier 4 | Above Rs. 10,000 crore |

## (a) Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

### LTV Limit
- **Maximum LTV: 75%** for all UCBs' lending against gold jewellery (including bullet repayment loans against pledge of gold jewellery)
- This applies uniformly across all tiers (Tier 1 through Tier 4)
- Per RBI circular RBI/2022-23/22 dated April 8, 2022

### Valuation Method
- Gold jewellery accepted as security/collateral must be valued at the **average of the closing price of 22 carat gold for the preceding 30 days** as quoted by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd. (IBJA)
- The LTV must be maintained at all times — if gold price falls, the UCB must obtain additional collateral or require partial repayment

### LTV Calculation Example
If gold jewellery valued at Rs. 10,00,000 (at 30-day average IBJA price):
- Maximum loan amount = 75% × Rs. 10,00,000 = **Rs. 7,50,000**

## (b) End-Use Monitoring

### Working Capital for Jewellery Manufacturers

Under the **RBI Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral Directions — 1st Amendment (September 29, 2025, RBI/2025-26/84)**:

1. **Tier 3 and Tier 4 UCBs** (along with Scheduled Commercial Banks) may extend **need-based working capital finance** to borrowers who use gold or silver as **raw material or input in manufacturing or industrial processing**
2. **Critical condition**: The borrower must NOT acquire or hold gold for investment or speculative purposes — the gold must genuinely be used as input in manufacturing
3. **End-use verification**: The UCB must verify:
   - The borrower is actually engaged in jewellery manufacturing (factory registration, GST registration showing manufacturing activity)
   - The gold pledged is raw material/work-in-progress, not held for speculation
   - Periodic checks (at least quarterly) on the manufacturing activity and stock records
4. **Prohibition**: UCBs cannot grant advances for the **purchase of gold in any form** — including primary gold, ornaments, jewellery, coins, gold ETFs, or mutual funds backed by gold. The finance must be for working capital needs, not for buying gold.

### Tier 1 and Tier 2 UCBs

- Tier 1 and Tier 2 UCBs are **NOT permitted** to extend working capital finance against gold/silver used as raw material — this concession applies only to Tier 3/4 UCBs and SCBs
- They can only offer regular gold loans (retail loans against pledge of gold jewellery)

## (c) Aggregate Exposure Limits — Tier 4 UCB Specific

### Single Borrower Exposure Limit
- Per RBI Master Direction on UCBs, the **single borrower exposure limit** for a Tier 4 UCB is **15% of the Tier I capital**
- For gold loans specifically, this general exposure limit applies — there is no separate lower limit for gold lending

### Group Borrower Exposure Limit
- **40% of Tier I capital** for group borrowers (including gold loans)

### Sectoral Exposure
- UCBs must ensure that total exposure to gold lending does not lead to concentration risk
- While there is no specific sectoral cap prescribed exclusively for gold lending, UCBs must comply with the overall **priority sector/non-priority sector** lending mix
- The Board of the UCB must set internal prudential limits for gold loan portfolio as a percentage of total advances

### Capital Adequacy Impact
- Gold loans carry a **risk weight of 50%** for capital adequacy (CRAR) calculation when LTV ≤ 75% (otherwise 100%)
- Tier 4 UCBs must maintain minimum CRAR of **12%** (higher than the 9% for Tier 1 UCBs)

### Bullet Repayment Loans
- Bullet repayment gold loans (full repayment at maturity) must not exceed **12 months** tenure
- For Tier 4 UCBs, the aggregate of bullet repayment gold loans should be monitored as part of asset-liability management
