  Where to Find/Create the Questions

  Tier 1: Your Own Data (Highest Signal)

  1. Recent circulars from your crawlers — Pick circulars from the last 2-3
  months (post-training cutoff for most LLMs). Formulate questions like "What
  are the new KYC requirements for NBFCs under RBI's Feb 2026 circular?" Generic
   LLMs literally can't answer these.
  2. Your obligations extraction output — You already extract compliance
  obligations. Turn them into questions: "What is the deadline for submitting
  XYZ return under SEBI's latest directive?" or "Which entity categories are
  exempt from this IRDAI requirement?"
  3. Cross-regulator questions from your corpus — Questions that span two
  regulators: "If my company is both SEBI-registered and RBI-regulated, which
  AML guidelines take precedence for customer due diligence?" These require
  synthesizing across your 10 crawler sources.

  Tier 2: Real Customer Pain Points

  4. Compliance officer interviews — Talk to 5 compliance officers (NBFCs,
  mid-size banks, insurance companies). Ask: "What was the last regulatory
  question you spent >1 hour researching?" Those are your benchmark questions.
  5. Regulatory penalty orders — RBI and SEBI publish enforcement actions. The
  violations reveal what companies actually get wrong. Frame questions around
  those: "What reporting frequency does an NBFC need to maintain under [specific
   circular] to avoid the penalty imposed in [enforcement order]?"
  6. Industry association webinars/events — FICCI, CII, ASSOCHAM host compliance
   webinars. The Q&A sections are gold mines. Check recordings or event
  summaries on YouTube/their websites.

  Tier 3: Online Communities

  7. Taxmann / CAClubIndia forums — Compliance professionals post real questions
   here. Filter for regulatory interpretation questions (not tax computation).
  8. LinkedIn compliance groups — Search for Indian regulatory compliance
  groups. Professionals share ambiguous situations they need help interpreting.
  9. IndiaCorpLaw blog — Academic analysis of regulatory changes. The edge cases
   they discuss make excellent benchmark questions.
  10. SEBI/RBI FAQ pages — Regulators publish FAQs in response to common
  confusion. If even the regulator needed to clarify it, it's a good test
  question.

  ---
  Question Design Strategy

  Design questions in 4 difficulty tiers to measure the moat precisely:

  Tier: Easy
  Type: Well-known regulation, basic facts
  Generic LLM Expected Score: ~80%
  Example: "What is the minimum capital requirement for an NBFC?"
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  Tier: Medium
  Type: Specific circular interpretation
  Generic LLM Expected Score: ~40-50%
  Example: "Under SEBI's Nov 2025 circular on mutual fund lite, what are the
    reduced compliance requirements?"
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  Tier: Hard
  Type: Entity-specific applicability
  Generic LLM Expected Score: ~20%
  Example: "My NBFC has AUM of 800cr. Does the scale-based regulation Tier 2
    apply, and what are the specific additional requirements vs Tier 1?"
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  Tier: Killer
  Type: Cross-regulator + recent + entity-specific
  Generic LLM Expected Score: ~5%
  Example: "My company received an IRDAI insurance broking license in Jan 2026
    and is also SEBI-registered. Which anti-money laundering reporting takes
    precedence, and what's the reconciliation process when the two frameworks
    conflict?"
